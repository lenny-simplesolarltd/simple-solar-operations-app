The Help Center holds short guides to how things work in this app. SimpleBot answers questions from the same guides, so both always say the same thing.

## Steps

1. Click **Help Center** in the menu on the left.
2. Type what you want to do in the search box, in your own words. For example "move a job", "customer cancelled" or "goods arrived".
3. Open the guide that fits best.

You can also browse **Common tasks**, **For your role** or a topic under **Browse by topic**.

## Help with the screen you are on

Click the **?** button at the top of any screen. It shows the guides for that screen. If there are none, search instead.

## Reading a guide

Most guides have the same parts:

- **Before you start**: anything you need first.
- **Steps**: what to click, in order.
- **What happens next**: what the app does afterwards.
- **If you can't do it**: the usual reasons, and what to do.

**Related** at the bottom lists guides that often come next.

> **Note:** A yellow box saying "This feature is not currently switched on" means the screen or button is not in use yet. Carry on the way you do today.

## If you can't find an answer

- Try fewer or different words.
- Ask SimpleBot. It searches the same guides.
- If a guide is missing or wrong, tell a manager so it can be corrected. The date at the bottom of each guide shows when it was last updated.
